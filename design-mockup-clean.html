Llewellyn Estate Heritage Conservation Area • 18 provisions
Heritage Controls Apply

This property is within the Llewellyn Estate HCA (Item I147). Development consent is required for external alterations or additions visible from the street.

O1

To conserve the heritage significance and special character of the Llewellyn Estate area.

C1

Retain and restore original roof forms, materials and chimneys. Slate or terracotta tiles preferred for roof replacement.

C2

Front fences and gates:

  1. Maximum height 1.2 m
  2. Maintain visual permeability through traditional picket or palisade designs
  3. Match original fence materials and detailing where evidence exists
C3

Additions and alterations to be setback from the primary façade and not dominate the original building form when viewed from the street.

C4

Retain mature street trees and established garden settings that contribute to the streetscape character.

HCA SIGNIFICANCE STATEMENT
Federation-era garden suburb development (1910s-1920s) characterised by detached cottages and bungalows with consistent setbacks, traditional materials, and garden settings creating a cohesive streetscape.

Precinct 15 — Petersham (North) • 12 controls
Precinct-Specific
Precinct Character Heritage Interface Victoria Road Corridor
O1

To maintain the established streetscape rhythm and building scale transitions between heritage areas and contemporary development.

C1

Front setbacks:

  1. Minimum 6 m from the front property boundary
  2. Align with predominant building line of adjoining properties
  3. Maintain visual continuity with existing streetscape rhythm
C2

Where the site adjoins a heritage item or HCA boundary, development is to respect the scale, setbacks and architectural character of the heritage context.

C3

Side setbacks minimum 0.9 m on both sides for single storey, increasing to 1.2 m for two storey development.

C4

Landscaped front setback areas are to include canopy trees and deep soil zones to soften built form and contribute to street tree canopy.

PRECINCT CHARACTER STATEMENT
Mixed heritage and interwar residential area along Victoria Road corridor with sensitive transitions to adjoining conservation areas. Predominant building form is single to two storey detached dwellings with consistent 6m front setbacks and established street tree canopy.
