# Screencast: Builder Quoting — Rear Extension + Second Storey in Heritage Area
## Ashfield R2 Residential Site — 90-120 seconds

---

### Case study setup (not on screen — narrator context)

**Dave Mitchell, Small-Medium Builder** (2-5 active projects, residential extensions/renovations)

Client wants a **rear extension + second storey addition** on their house in Ashfield heritage area.

**Dave's quoting checklist:**
1. **Rear setback:** How far back can I build? (affects floor area, quote price)
2. **Height limit + solar access planes:** Does second storey comply? Will I need costly stepped roof?
3. **Heritage materials:** Can I use modern brick/cladding or must match existing face brick? (2x cost difference)
4. **Heritage Additions controls:** Must addition be hidden/subservient or can it be prominent?

**His manual process:**
- Download Ashfield DCP (200+ pages across multiple PDFs)
- Find rear setback rules (Part B or Part F? Varies by dwelling type)
- Find height controls (LEP) and solar access diagrams (DCP Part B — complex 3D diagrams)
- Find heritage materials requirements (Part C — 50+ pages, must read carefully)
- Calculate buildable envelope based on all constraints
- Price materials: heritage brick ($150/sqm) vs standard brick ($80/sqm)
- **Time:** 1-2 hours per quote
- **Risk:** Miss one control = underquote = $5k-20k loss on variations

**With PlotDetect:** 10 minutes to see all setbacks, height, solar access, heritage materials → accurate quote.
