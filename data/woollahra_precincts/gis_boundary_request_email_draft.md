# Woollahra DCP boundary request — draft email (send from your account)

**To:** records@woollahra.nsw.gov.au (please route to the GIS / strategic planning team)
**Subject:** Woollahra DCP 2015 — GIS extents for Part B1 residential precincts and D-series centres

---

Hello,

I'm building a property research tool that maps which parts of the Woollahra DCP 2015 apply to which land. The heritage conservation area boundaries are already available through the NSW Planning Portal's LEP layers, which cover the Chapter C and B2 areas well.

Two sets of DCP-defined areas don't appear in any published layer I can find (including Council's ArcGIS Online content):

1. the **Chapter B1 residential precincts** (B1.2 Darling Point through B1.11, as mapped in the precinct figures of Chapter B1), and
2. the **Chapter D centre boundaries** — the D1 neighbourhood centres, D2 mixed use centres, D4 Edgecliff Centre and D6 Rose Bay Centre map extents.

Could you let me know whether these exist as GIS data (shapefile / GeoJSON / a feature service, any format is fine), and if so whether Council can share them or publish them? If they only exist as the adopted PDF figures, a pointer to the mapping that produced those figures would also help.

Happy to explain the use case — the aim is simply that people looking up a property see only the precinct controls that actually apply to it.

Thanks and regards,
Lawrence McDonell
