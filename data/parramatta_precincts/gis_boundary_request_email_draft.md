**Subject:** Parramatta DCP 2023 — GIS extents for the Part 8 precincts and special character areas

Hello,

I'm building a property research tool that maps which parts of the Parramatta DCP 2023 apply to which land. Council's published GIS has been genuinely useful — the City Centre late night trading layer is even keyed to the DCP part references, which is exactly the right shape for this.

The one set of DCP-defined areas I can't find in any published layer is **Part 8**: the strategic and local centre precincts (Epping, Westmead, Parramatta North, Granville, Camellia & Rydalmere, Newington, Melrose Park, Telopea, Carlingford), the Part 8.3 neighbourhood precincts, the Part 8.4 special character areas, and the Part 8.5 specific sites — as mapped in the Part 8 figures.

Could you let me know whether these extents exist as GIS data (shapefile / GeoJSON / a feature service, any format is fine), and whether Council can share or publish them — ideally the same way as the late night trading layer? If they only exist as the adopted PDF figures, a pointer to the source mapping would also help.

Happy to explain the use case — the aim is simply that someone looking up a property sees only the precinct controls that actually apply to it.

Thanks and regards,
Lawrence McDonell
