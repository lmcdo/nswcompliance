 11. UX Best Practices for Planners

 DO:
 - Show zone immediately after address entry
 - Pre-fill known values (reduce typing)
 - One-click provision copying (for reports)
 - Auto-save drafts (planners get interrupted)
 - Clear visual hierarchy (critical items first)

 DON'T:
 - Hide version information
 - Require multiple clicks for common tasks
 - Use technical database terms
 - Force sequential workflows
 - Timeout sessions quickly (assessments take time)

 This UI/UX design leverages your current database
 capabilities while providing the workflow efficiency that
 council planners desperately need. The focus is on reducing
 assessment time from 30-45 minutes to 10-15 minutes per
 application.
