# Screencast: Architect Concept Design — Rear Extension + Second Storey
## Ashfield R2 Heritage Site — 90-120 seconds

---

### Case study setup (not on screen — narrator context)

**Lisa Tran, Residential Architect** (boutique practice, 5-10 active projects)

Client wants concept design for **rear extension + second storey addition** on their Ashfield heritage property.

**Lisa's concept design checklist (buildable envelope):**
1. **FSR limit:** What's the max floor area I can design? (LEP control)
2. **Height limit:** What's the max building height? (LEP control)
3. **Setbacks:** How far from boundaries? Defines buildable footprint (DCP control)
4. **Solar access planes:** Does second storey need to step back? (DCP control)
5. **Heritage constraints:** Can I do contemporary design or must replicate heritage style? (DCP control)

**Client expectation:** First concept presentation in 1 week (preliminary sketches + 3D renders)

**Lisa's manual process:**
- Download Ashfield DCP (Part B, Part C, Part F)
- Check LEP for FSR and height limits (Planning Portal or legislation.nsw.gov.au)
- Find setback requirements (scattered across Part B and Part F)
- Find solar access plane diagrams (Part B — complex 2D diagrams)
- Read Part C heritage provisions (50 pages) to understand design constraints
- Calculate buildable envelope: max floor area, max footprint, max height
- **Time:** 1-2 hours per concept
- **Cost:** $200-800 in billable time (at $200-400/hr)
- **Risk:** Design envelope wrong → client unhappy → redesign → delay

**With PlotDetect:** 5-10 minutes to get FSR, height, setbacks, heritage constraints → define envelope → start designing.
