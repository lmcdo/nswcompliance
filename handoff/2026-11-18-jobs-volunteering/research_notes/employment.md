# Employment market for a Python/TS/PostGIS/geospatial/LLM-extraction developer with NSW planning domain knowledge (Sydney/NSW + remote Australia, as of 18 Nov 2026)

> **Method caveat (read first):** Searches ran on 18 Nov 2026. Direct page fetches were blocked by this session's network policy for `ia.acs.org.au` and `au.seek.com`, so most findings below come from search-engine result summaries, not full reads of the primary pages. Figures should be checked against the linked source before being quoted. ~19 tool calls used; many sub-questions (Levels.fyi, Jora, Robert Half, Hudson, APS/DTA, LinkedIn response rates, named councils/consultancies) were not reached and are listed as Gaps.

## 1. State of the Australian/NSW tech job market, late 2026 (demand, AI impact on junior/mid, contract vs perm, salaries and day rates)

### Takeaway
The overall market is soft: SEEK job ads fell for about a year before ticking up in Aug and Sep 2026 (still −3.5% YoY in September), the ICT industry reportedly shrank in 2026, and big Australian software employers (WiseTech, Atlassian, Canva) made large cuts. AI-engineer roles pay a premium: about A$140k mid / A$170k senior base in Sydney, and roughly A$800–1,150/day on contract in NSW. Entry-level developer postings are under the most pressure.

The October 2026 report (normally out mid-November) was not yet indexed when searched — [Staffing Industry Analysts](https://www.staffingindustry.com/news/global-daily-news/australian-job-ads-edge-up-06-in-september)

Contract medians are $560/day (graduate), $923 (mid), $1,120 (senior) and $1,252 (lead). Senior "AI Engineer (LLM)" contract median is $886/day — [Galileo](https://galileosearch.com.au/ai-engineer-salary-australia); [Galileo LLM](https://salary.galileosearch.com.au/salary/ai-engineer-llm); [Data Engineer (AI)](https://salary.galileosearch.com.au/salary/data-engineer-ai)

- Recruiter guides with commercial interest quote higher figures: senior AI engineer A$170–230k+, and GenAI contract rates of $1,000–2,200+/day — [AI Talent on Demand](https://www.aitalentondemand.com.au/article/generative-ai-engineer-salary-australia-2026); [Lightning Ventures (~10 months old)](https://www.lightningventures.com.au/talent/guides/ai-engineer-salary-guide-australia/)

**GIS salaries (much lower than AI/SWE)**
- Payscale (self-reported, sample and dates unclear): GIS Analyst, Sydney, A$64–96k base. Geospatial Analyst, Sydney, average about A$80k (2026). Experienced Geospatial Analyst, Australia, median A$102k (p10 $71k, p90 $138k) — [Payscale GIS Sydney](https://www.payscale.com/research/AU/Job=Geographic_Information_Systems_(GIS)_Analyst/Salary/01cd4d9b/Sydney); [Payscale Geospatial Experienced](https://www.payscale.com/research/AU/Job=Geospatial_Analyst/Salary/73767303/Experienced)
- NSW public-sector benchmark: DPHI Senior Application Developer (Spatial), permanent, **A$133,348–146,945 + super**, closed 17 Sep 2026 — [freehire aggregator](https://freehire.me/jobs/senior-application-developer-department-of-planning-housing-and-infrastructure-nsw-emtj2syl)

### Inferences
- Pay depends heavily on the title the candidate is hired under. As an "AI engineer / applied AI" the profile prices at about A$140–170k or A$900–1,100/day. As a "GIS analyst" it prices at about A$80–100k. Candidates should position as an applied-AI / data engineer with a geospatial specialty, not as a GIS analyst.
- Big local software employers are cutting engineers. The likely demand pockets are government digital programs, AI-adoption roles, and niche domain vendors.
- A mid-career person with a shipped production system is less exposed to the junior squeeze than graduates, but "years of commercial experience" filters will still bite if that history is not documented.

### Gaps
- October 2026 SEEK report, JSA IVI ICT counts, the JSA 2026 Occupation Shortage List entries (e.g. whether Software Engineer, Developer Programmer or GIS-related occupations are in shortage) — not retrieved.
- No Levels.fyi, Jora, Robert Half or Hudson 2026 figures were retrieved. No Hays role-level bands. No general full-stack or data-engineer contract rates for NSW.

## 3. AI-specific roles and freelance AI automation demand/rates

### Takeaway
Employed or contract AI engineer roles in Sydney pay well (see section 1). Open-marketplace freelance AI automation work is heavily price-compressed by global competition (US$20–60/hr typical posted budgets). Australia-based listings run higher (one at A$80–130/hr). Expert360 targets consulting and fractional-executive work, not hands-on builders.

### Cited Findings
- Upwork posted budgets: worldwide AI-automation and agent jobs often post US$30–60/hr (expert) or $19–40/hr (intermediate). An Australia-based Power Platform/Azure Document Intelligence contract posted **A$80–130/hr** for 3–6 months part-time — [Upwork AU listing](https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/Australian-Power-Platform-Contractor-Power-Automate-SharePoint-Azure-Document-Intelligence_~022067104751174188207/); [Upwork "Automation Engineer – Australia-based"](https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/Automation-Engineer-Mid-senior-Australia-based_~022066506002270046345/)
- Example Upwork demand matching the profile: "Build an AI Resume Intake Agent using Claude API + Gmail MCP" — [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/jobs/~022064869284886852246); "Senior AI Engineer (LLM Agents, RAG & Automation)" — [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/Senior-Engineer-LLM-Agents-RAG-Automation_~022085080433386011634/)
- A vendor estimate (commercially motivated) puts 2026 Upwork rates at $25–50/hr for junior automation (n8n/Zapier) and $50–100/hr for mid-level LLM/RAG — [Ad Snipper](https://adsnipper.com/?p=2400)
- Expert360 focuses on management consultants, finance/strategy specialists and fractional executives in ANZ. A report that Swipejobs was acquiring Expert360 (Oct 2026) is **unverified** — per search summary; no primary source retrieved
- PwC 2026 Global AI Jobs Barometer: workers with AI skills earn a 62% wage premium (global/US-weighted figure, may not transfer to AU) — cited in [dev.to](https://dev.to/shakeelahmed/ai-layoffs-2026-which-jobs-are-actually-being-cut-and-which-ones-are-hiring-434g) (secondary)

### Inferences
- Contracting through AU agencies or direct to AU clients (about A$900+/day equivalent) beats global marketplaces by roughly 3–5x per hour for this profile.
- "LLM document extraction with QA/evaluation discipline" maps onto the scarcer, higher-paid end of AI work (evals and reliability) rather than commodity n8n/Zapier builds. This is an inference; no AU data on eval-specific roles was found.

### Gaps

## 4. NSW Government hiring channels and how contractors get on panels

### Takeaway
Permanent and temporary NSW roles are advertised on iworkfor.nsw.gov.au (aggregators mirror them). Contractors reach agencies in two ways. Individuals usually go through a recruitment agency prequalified on the mandatory **Contingent Workforce Scheme (SCM0007)**. A sole-trader or small company can register on buy.nsw and join the **ICT Services Scheme**, which is open to new suppliers at any time until 28 Feb 2029.

### Cited Findings
- ICT Services Scheme: suppliers can apply at any time and choose service categories. Agencies **must** buy ICT from scheme suppliers. A buy.nsw account (buy.nsw.gov.au/login/signup) is required first. Membership runs to 28 Feb 2029. Contact: ICTServices@customerservice.nsw.gov.au — [buy.nsw ICT Services Scheme](https://www.info.buy.nsw.gov.au/schemes/ict-services-scheme); [supplier guidance](https://www.info.buy.nsw.gov.au/supplier-guidance/supply-to-government)
- Contingent Workforce Scheme (SCM0007): mandatory and extended to 28 Feb 2028. Sources cite PBD 2012-02 or PBD-2021-04 (conflict noted). Suppliers are prequalified, a managed service provider sources workers under Contract C0008, and rates are standardised — [buy.nsw CWS](https://info.buy.nsw.gov.au/schemes/contingent-workforce-scheme); [C0008 MSP scope](https://www.info.buy.nsw.gov.au/contracts/c0008-managed-services-provider/scope); [Notre Dame procurement page](https://www.notredame.edu.au/staff/support/finance/procurement/how-to-buy/engaging-short-term-contingent-workers/new-south-wales-contractors)
- NSW Department of Education policy requires extra approval when a contractor's proposed rate exceeds **A$1,110/day**. This is a threshold, not a rate card, and the policy date is unclear — [NSW DoE contingent labour policy](https://education.nsw.gov.au/policy-library/policies/pd-2024-0484-03)
- PSC Contingent Workforce Management Guidelines (2020, older) — [PSC NSW](https://www.psc.nsw.gov.au/assets/psc/documents/Contingent-Workforce-Management-Guidelines-2020.pdf); Contractor Central resource — [buy.nsw](https://www.info.buy.nsw.gov.au/resources/contractor-central)

### Inferences
- The A$1,110/day approval threshold implies most NSW Government developer contracts are priced at or below roughly A$1,100/day paid to the worker.
- For an individual, the fastest route into NSW government contract work is registering with recruitment agencies on the CWS supplier list. Joining the ICT Services Scheme directly is better suited to fixed-scope deliverables (e.g. a data or AI extraction project) sold by a sole-trader company.

### Gaps
- No current CWS supplier list was found (e.g. whether Talent International, Hays or Randstad are on it), and no published rate card.
- APS Jobs, the Digital Transformation Agency and the federal Digital Marketplace were not researched.

## 5. What evidence says helps candidates now (portfolios, OSS, referrals, outreach, recruiters, age)

### Takeaway
Hard Australian evidence is thin. The best available data says senior developers weight practical project experience most, developers see GitHub as the best proof of skill, and referrals and networks fill many AU tech roles (vendor claims). Age bias is real and measured. AHRI/AHRC data shows HR professionals increasingly class people in their early 50s as "older", and only 1% think older workers have better tech skills.

### Cited Findings
- Seniors weight practical project experience (70%) over internships (56%) when assessing juniors (BairesDev survey) — [IT Brief](https://itbrief.com.au/story/ai-tools-widen-gap-in-junior-software-engineers-skills)
- daily.dev survey of 4,040 developers in 177 countries (2025): 37% said GitHub best represents real skills, against 17% for personal portfolio and 14% for LinkedIn — [daily.dev](https://daily.dev/blog/where-developers-look-for-jobs-2026/)
- An AU-focused guide claims the best AU tech roles in 2026 "rarely make it to job boards" and are filled via networks and referrals. This is a vendor blog with no data — [jobrise](https://jobrise.io/en/blog/entry-level-software-jobs-in-australia-how-to-get-hired-in-2026/)
- AHRI + Australian Human Rights Commission, *Older and Younger Workers* (148 HR professionals surveyed Mar–May 2025, the latest found): the share labelling workers in their early 50s as "older" rose to 24% (from 10% in 2023). Only 1% said older workers have better tech skills. In 2023, "lack of tech skills" was a top-3 barrier to hiring older workers (22%) — [AHRI](https://www.ahri.com.au/articles/why-our-definition-of-an-older-worker-is-shifting); [AHRC media release](https://humanrights.gov.au/about-us/media-centre/search-listing-media-releases/employer-biases-against-older-and-younger-generations-hindering); [The Nightly](https://thenightly.com.au/business/ageism-growing-with-australian-employers-now-classifying-workers-over-50-as-older-c-19424604)
- University of Melbourne research: AI hiring tools may screen out applicants over 45 — [Pursuit (UniMelb)](https://pursuit.unimelb.edu.au/articles/over-45-and-looking-for-a-job-ai-thinks-you-might-be-too-old)
- 18% of HR professionals were "not open at all" to hiring people aged 65+ (figures vary by survey year and age bracket) — [HCA Mag](https://www.hcamag.com/au/specialisation/diversity-inclusion/australian-employers-actively-excluding-jobseekers-above-65/444772)
- Job postings increasingly ask candidates to show their AI tool workflow — [Dynamic Business](https://dynamicbusiness.com/news/show-your-work-flow-why-modern-job-postings-need-an-ai-tool-callout)

### Inferences
- For an experienced career-changer, a public, inspectable production system (repo, live demo, eval reports) directly counters the "lacks tech skills" bias AHRI measured. Applying through warm introductions also avoids the automated screening where age-correlated filtering is reported.
- Demonstrating Claude Code / agentic workflow explicitly matches the emerging "show your AI workflow" posting trend.

### Gaps
- No AU data was found on LinkedIn cold-outreach response rates, recruiter placement share, or open-source contributions as a hiring factor.
- No 2026 AU recruiter survey was found on AI-generated applications or ATS screening.
- The "42% of mature workers report age discrimination" figure (IT Brief citing AHRI) could not be verified against the primary report.
