# PlotDetect: Executive Summary for Council Stakeholders

**One-Page Overview** | **Version**: 2.0 | **Date**: January 2026

---

## What Is PlotDetect?

A **compliance workflow tool** that organizes NSW planning provisions (SEPPs, LEPs, DCPs) for property certifiers, following industry standards from legal research platforms like LexisNexis.

**Critical Distinction**: We **organize and prioritize** provisions for efficient review. We do **NOT filter or automate compliance decisions**. Professional certifiers retain full responsibility.

---

## Core Architecture: 4-Layer Applicability Model

Layer 1: GENERIC - All properties (100%)
Layer 2: ZONE-SPECIFIC - Based on zoning (e.g., R2)
Layer 3: CONDITION - Heritage, flood, bushfire, etc.
Layer 4: PRECINCT - Geographic location

**Result**: 400-700 applicable provisions per property (vs 47,818 total)

---

## Industry Standard: The LexisNexis Model

**LexisNexis**: Shows ALL law → Tags help navigation → Professional reviews EVERYTHING

**PlotDetect**: Shows ALL provisions → Tags help prioritization → Certifier reviews EVERYTHING

**Key Principle**: Organization tool, not decision tool.

---

## Data Quality: 99% structural, 95% content accuracy

**Safeguard**: All provisions shown regardless of categorization confidence
