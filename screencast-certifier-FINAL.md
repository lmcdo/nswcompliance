**David's REAL question:**
"Is CDC pathway available or does this require DA?"

**Why this matters:**
- **CDC application:** $8k-12k fee, 20-day approval, certifier signs off

- **Certifier's risk:** If CDC issued incorrectly → council enforcement → professional indemnity claim

**Manual process:** Download LEP (check Part 6 for site-specific clauses), check SEPP Housing 2021 Schedule 2 CDC standards, check site for heritage/flood. **1-2 hours. Miss Part 6 clause = issue invalid CDC.**
