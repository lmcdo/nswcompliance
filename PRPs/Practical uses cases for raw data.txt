 Why This Actually Works

 1. Realistic Value Proposition
 - Not claiming to replace professionals
 - Genuinely saves time and reduces confusion
 - Clear about limitations

 2. Defensible Business Model
 - Research tool, not advice
 - Professional verification always recommended
 - Users make informed decisions

 3. Scalable with Imperfect Data
 - Document navigation doesn't require perfect extraction
 - Red flag detection works with keyword matching
 - Complexity scoring improves over time

 Revenue Potential

 Target Segments:
 - Planning consultants: $50-200/month for research efficiency
 - Property developers: $500-2000/month for portfolio analysis
 - Architects: $100-500/month for preliminary assessments
 - Property lawyers: $200-800/month for due diligence

 Value: Even saving 2-3 hours per project = $300-900 in professional
 time

 Bottom Line

 Your data is most valuable as a navigation and triage tool, not a
 compliance engine. The business opportunity is in making the planning
 system more accessible, not in replacing professional judgment.

 Users pay for efficiency and risk reduction, not for definitive
 answers they can't legally rely on anyway.
