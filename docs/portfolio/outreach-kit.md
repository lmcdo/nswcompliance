# Outreach Kit

Income first. Every email exists to start a conversation, not to close a
deal. Attach/link: the case study, the sample report (once made), and the
public prose-gate repo. Nothing else.

## Positioning

**One-liner** (bios, profiles, signatures):
> I build verification layers for AI systems working over regulatory
> data — deterministic pipelines where every answer cites its source
> clause and the model never gets the last word.

**Paragraph** (proposals, About pages):
> I run a production planning-compliance engine for NSW that serves
> 47,000+ regulatory provisions with per-clause source references and
> effective dates. Its rule: AI assists extraction, but never generates
> an answer — every served value is mechanically re-verified against its
> own quoted source before release. I take contract work applying the
> same discipline to other teams' pipelines: AI-output QA, provenance
> gates, and regulatory data extraction that can survive an audit.

## Prospect list criteria (build to 30)

1. **NSW councils in the Early Adopter grant program** (16 councils,
   publicly listed) — the digital/planning transformation lead, not the
   general inbox.
2. **Vendors on the NSW AI Solutions Panel** — partnerships/product
   leads. You are a verification component, not a competitor.
3. **Rules-as-code consultancies** (Salsa Digital and peers, incl. NZ) —
   they staff Python people who can read legislation; you are a rare
   direct fit.
4. **Proptech/regtech/legaltech firms shipping LLM features** over
   planning, title, conveyancing or compliance data (AU first, then UK —
   similar planning-system shape).
5. **PI insurers / certifier bodies** exploring AI exposure (slower
   burn; one conversation is market research even if no contract).

## Cadence & hygiene

- 5 personalised sends/week — quality over volume; a template email that
  reads like one is deleted.
- One follow-up per prospect at +5 business days (two sentences, add one
  new piece of value — e.g. the case study if the first email didn't
  link it). Then stop; silence is an answer.
- Log every send/reply in one spreadsheet: date, org, person, template,
  outcome. Review conversion by template monthly and rewrite the losers.
- Never claim what can't be shown in 5 minutes on a call. The demo
  assets exist so every sentence in these emails is checkable.
