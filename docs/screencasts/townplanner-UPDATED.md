### Case study setup (not on screen — narrator context)

**Emma Roberts, Town Planner** at boutique planning consultancy (3-5 DAs per week)

Preparing **Development Application (DA) for dual occupancy** on Ashfield R2 heritage site.

**Her DA report checklist (provisions to cite):**
1. **LEP Controls:** FSR, height, heritage (Clauses 4.4, 4.3, 5.10, 6.20)
2. **SEPP Requirements:** BASIX, parking (if applicable), design standards
3. **DCP Controls:** Setbacks, heritage design, parking, landscaping, waste

**EP&A Act s 4.15 requires ALL three** (LEP + SEPP + DCP) to be cited in DA report.

**Her manual process:**
- Download Inner West LEP 2022 (200+ pages)
- Download SEPP Sustainable Buildings 2022, SEPP Housing 2021
- Download Ashfield DCP (Part A, B, C, D, F, Chapter A)
- Find applicable provisions in each document
- Copy provision text into DA report template
- Manually note PDF page citations
- Cross-check nothing missed
- **Time:** 4-6 hours per DA
- **Cost:** $600-1,800 in billable time (at $150-300/hr)
- **Risk:** Miss SEPP provision → council RFI → 2-week delay

**With PlotDetect:** 30 minutes to see all provisions organized by DA section, copy into report with citations.
