# Product Demo — 90 Seconds — Spoken with Talking Head
## verify.plotdetect.com.au
## Format: Narrator head bottom-right corner. Narration carries thread. Overlays reinforce key data only.
## Audience: Architects, town planners, developers, council officers

---

### Framing principle
Tone: peer to peer, not sales. Speak as a practitioner who found a better tool.
Domain knowledge assumed — no explaining fundamentals.
Narrator connects the three layers as a progressive answer to one question:
"What do I need to know about this site before I start?"

### Production notes

**Talking head setup:**
- Bottom-right corner, small circle crop, during screen action
- Full frame or larger at 1:20 for sign-off
- Neutral background or office — nothing distracting
- Tone: practitioner, not sales. Conversational pace, not rushed.

**Overlay use:** Sparse. Only when reinforcing a number or term the narrator just said. Don't duplicate full sentences.

**Clause 6.20 moment (0:34):** This is the emotional peak of the video — the "aha" that a practitioner will recognise. Don't rush it. Slow mouse, badge appears, 2-second hold before narrator continues.

**Practise the triage click:** Hover → × → reason field → type → confirm in under 4 seconds or it kills the pace.

**Pre-recording setup:**
- Session pre-loaded with dev type set and 3–4 provisions marked
- PDF export must show substantive content — pre-mark at least: 2 Complies, 1 Varies, 1 N/A

**Total: 90 seconds.**
