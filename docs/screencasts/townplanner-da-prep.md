# Screencast: Town Planner DA Preparation — Dual Occupancy in Heritage Area
## Ashfield R2 Site — 90-120 seconds

---

### Case study setup (not on screen — narrator context)

**Emma Roberts, Town Planner** at a boutique planning consultancy (3-5 DAs per week)

She's preparing a **Development Application (DA) for dual occupancy** on a site in Ashfield R2 zone with heritage overlay.

**Her DA report checklist (provisions to cite):**
1. **Built Form:** Setbacks, height, building design controls
2. **Parking & Access:** Car parking rates, driveway design, access requirements
3. **Landscaping:** Deep soil, landscaped area, tree preservation
4. **Heritage Impact:** All heritage controls (Materials, Additions, Demolition, Character, Fencing)
5. **Privacy & Amenity:** Privacy screens, solar access, overlooking
6. **Waste Management:** Bin storage, collection access
7. **Stormwater:** Drainage, stormwater management

Each section of the DA report must cite specific DCP provisions with PDF page references.

**Her manual process:**
- Download Ashfield DCP (200+ pages across Part A, Part B, Part C, Part D, Part F, Chapter A)
- Read through each relevant section to find applicable provisions
- Copy provision text into DA report template (Microsoft Word)
- Manually note PDF page citations (e.g., "Part C, page 18")
- Cross-check all provisions found (easy to miss provisions buried in text)
- **Time:** 4-6 hours per DA
- **Cost:** $600-1,800 in billable time (at $150-300/hr)

**With PlotDetect:** 30 minutes to see all provisions organized by DA section, copy into report with PDF citations.
