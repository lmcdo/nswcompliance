## **Phase 2: Monetization Strategy**

### **Tiered Model (Recommended)**

**FREE Tier - "Homeowner"**
- 5 property lookups/month
- Basic compliance check (LEP + DCP only)
- No AI chat, no API access
- Watermarked PDF reports

**PRO Tier - $149/month - "Professional"**
- Unlimited property lookups
- Full compliance (LEP + DCP + SEPP + Heritage + Flood)
- AI chat (50 questions/month)
- Downloadable reports (no watermark)
- Email support

**ENTERPRISE Tier - $499/month - "Firm"**
- Everything in Pro
- API access (10,000 calls/month)
- White-label reports (your firm's logo)
- Priority support (24hr response)
- Multi-user accounts (5 seats)

**API-Only Tier - $0.02/call**
- For software integrations (Archistar, Simblicity, etc.)
- Volume discounts at 100k+ calls/month
- No UI access

### **Why This Works**
- **Free tier drives adoption** (homeowners become word-of-mouth marketing)
- **Pro tier is impulse buy** ($149 = cost of 1 hour billable time saved)
- **Enterprise captures firms** (certifiers billing $200-400/hr can justify easily)
- **API opens B2B** (PropTech companies will pay for integration)

## **Phase 3: Expansion Strategy (Sydney → NSW)**

### **LGA Expansion Roadmap**
**Phase 1 (Current):** Inner West (proven, 7,050 provisions)
**Phase 2 (Months 1-3):** Add 3 high-value LGAs
- **Waverley** (high-density, complex heritage, wealthy demographic)
- **Randwick** (university area, high DA volume, SEPP Design Guide heavy)
- **Willoughby** (North Shore, affluent, multi-unit dev market)

**Why these?**
- High development activity (100+ DAs/month each)
- Complex overlays (heritage, flooding, SEPP Housing)
- Wealthy residents = willingness to pay for compliance tools
- Geographic diversity = test different DCP structures

**Phase 3 (Months 4-9):** Expand to 10 more Sydney LGAs
- Canterbury-Bankstown, Parramatta, Ryde, Bayside, Sutherland, Lane Cove, Mosman, Woollahra, Canada Bay, Strathfield

**Phase 4 (Year 2):** Regional NSW
- Newcastle, Wollongong, Central Coast (use state SEPP data, simpler DCPs)

### **Data Acquisition Strategy**
- **Scrape council websites** (DCP PDFs publicly available)
- **Use Planning Portal API** for all spatial data (already integrated)
- **Manual georeferencing in QGIS** for precinct boundaries from DCP PDFs
- **Hire law student** to manually tag provisions ($25/hr, 40hr per LGA)

**Cost per LGA:** ~$1,500 (data processing + QA)
**Revenue break-even:** 10 Pro subscriptions per LGA
