## The Opportunity

### Inner West's Specific Problem
- **3 unconsolidated DCPs** still in effect (Leichhardt, Marrickville, Ashfield)
- **LEP consolidated** in 2022, but DCPs are NOT
- Each DCP has different precinct structures, provision numbering
- Staff must know which DCP applies, then find the right section
- Applicants often miss provisions, leading to incomplete submissions
- Consolidated DCP is 2+ years away based on other councils' experience

### Market Context
- NSW Government invested $5.6M in AI for planning (AI Solutions Panel)
- 30% of DAs require additional info, adding 42 days average
- PropCode and DAISY are on the panel but don't handle merged council complexity
- Inner West has expressed interest in AI/automation

### Goal
Get a 30-minute demo meeting

### Opening Script
> "Hi, I'm [name] from Solvyra. I've built a tool that helps planning staff and applicants navigate Inner West's three separate DCPs more efficiently. Given the council hasn't yet consolidated the Leichhardt, Marrickville, and Ashfield DCPs, I think this could save significant assessment time. I'm wondering if there's someone in Planning or Service Transformation I could show this to for 30 minutes?"

### Key Phrases to Use
- "Three separate DCPs" (shows you understand their specific problem)
- "Pre-lodgement self-assessment" (aligns with NSW Government priority)
- "Reduce incomplete submissions" (speaks to their pain)

### Likely Responses and Rebuttals

**Response 1: "What exactly does it do?"**
> "It lets you enter any Inner West address and instantly see all applicable provisions from the correct DCP - Leichhardt, Marrickville, or Ashfield - with precinct-specific controls. For applicants, it's a self-service tool that helps them address requirements before lodging. For staff, it's a quick lookup across all three DCPs without hunting through PDFs."

**Response 2: "We're looking at the AI Solutions Panel products"**
> "I'm familiar with DAISY and PropCode on the panel. They're great for DA validation and CDC eligibility. I've built the provision data layer that those tools need but don't have for merged councils."

**Response 3: "We don't have budget for this"**
> "I understand. I'm not proposing a big procurement. I'm suggesting a pilot - perhaps just making this available to your pre-lodgement team to test. If it reduces incomplete submissions and saves assessment time, we can discuss formal arrangements. If it doesn't work, no cost to you."

**Response 4: "Send me an email with details"**

## Objection Handling

### "We're waiting for our consolidated DCP"
> "I understand. That could be 2+ years away based on other councils' experience. In the meantime, staff and applicants still need to navigate three separate DCPs. This bridges the gap. And when you do consolidate, the extracted provisions could help inform that process."

### "We need to go through procurement"
> "I appreciate that. For a pilot with under $5,000 value, does that typically require full procurement? If so, I'm happy to work through your process. Alternatively, could this be positioned as a trial/evaluation before procurement?"

### "How do we know this is accurate?"
> "The provisions are extracted directly from your current DCPs on the Planning Portal. I can show you the source PDF page for any provision. I'd suggest your planners verify a sample during the pilot. If they find errors, I fix them immediately."

### "What about data security?"
> "The provision data comes from your publicly available DCPs. No personal or sensitive data is involved. The application runs on Vercel (AWS infrastructure) with standard security. For a formal arrangement, I'm happy to complete your security questionnaire."

### "What happens if you disappear?"
> "Fair question. The extracted provision data could be exported to you. For a formal arrangement, we could include data portability clauses. But I'm committed to this space - Inner West is just the start, I'm planning to expand to other merged councils."

### "PropCode/DAISY already does this"
> "They're excellent tools - PropCode for CDC eligibility, DAISY for document validation. What they don't have is Inner West's precinct-level provision filtering. PropCode treats each council separately without understanding merged council complexity. I've built specifically for that gap."
