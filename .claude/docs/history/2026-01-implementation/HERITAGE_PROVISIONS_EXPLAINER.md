# Heritage Provisions: When They Apply and Why

## Overview

Heritage development controls in Inner West LGA come from two sources with different triggers:

1. **LEP Heritage Controls** → Based on LEP heritage status (HCA or Heritage Item)
2. **DCP Heritage Controls** → Can apply based on EITHER LEP status OR precinct location

This creates a situation where **properties that are NOT in a Heritage Conservation Area may still receive heritage provisions** from the DCP.

---

## The Planning Hierarchy

### Level 1: Environmental Planning & Assessment Act 1979 (EP&A Act)

**Section 3.42**: DCPs supplement LEPs

Key principle:
> Development Control Plans supplement Local Environmental Plans by providing detailed planning and design guidelines to support the policy framework established in the LEP.

**What this means**:
- LEPs establish **what** (zones, land uses, heritage items/areas)
- DCPs establish **how** (design controls, building requirements)
- DCPs can apply controls based on geographic areas (precincts) independently of LEP schedules

### Level 2: Inner West Local Environmental Plan 2022 (LEP)

**Clause 5.10**: Heritage Conservation Areas and Heritage Items

The LEP:
- **Defines** which properties are Heritage Items
- **Maps** Heritage Conservation Areas (HCAs)
- **Requires** consent for development affecting heritage
- **Does NOT provide detailed design controls** (that's the DCP's job)

### Level 3: Development Control Plan (DCP)

The DCP provides two types of heritage controls:

#### Type 1: Generic Heritage Controls
**Authority**: LEP Clause 5.10 (heritage status)

**Location in DCP**:
- Leichhardt: Part C Section 1.4 - "Heritage Conservation Areas and Heritage Items"
- Ashfield: Chapter E1 - Heritage provisions
- Marrickville: Part 8 - Heritage provisions

**Scope Statement** (Leichhardt Part C Section 1.4, Page 15):
> "This element outlines objectives and controls for the development and conservation of buildings **within Heritage Conservation Areas and Heritage Items**."

**Trigger**: Property IS listed in LEP Schedule 5 OR IS within a mapped HCA

**Application**: Generic heritage provisions apply **only to properties with LEP heritage status**

#### Type 2: Precinct-Specific Heritage Controls
**Authority**: EP&A Act s 3.42 (DCP precinct maps define scope)

**Location in DCP**:
- Leichhardt: Part C Section 2 - Distinctive Neighbourhoods (8 precincts with heritage provisions)
- Ashfield: Chapter D - Precincts (7 precincts with heritage provisions)
- Marrickville: Part 9 - Precincts (41 precincts with heritage provisions)

**Scope**: Defined by precinct boundary maps, NOT LEP heritage schedules

**Trigger**: Property IS within precinct boundary (shown on precinct maps)

**Application**: Precinct provisions apply **to ALL properties within the precinct boundary**, regardless of whether the property has LEP heritage status

## Why This Matters: The Legal Authority

### Question:
*"If a property is NOT in a Heritage Conservation Area, why does it receive heritage provisions from the DCP?"*

### Answer:

**Because DCP precincts are NOT the same as LEP Heritage Conservation Areas.**

The confusion arises because:
1. Some precincts contain Heritage Conservation Areas
2. Some precincts have heritage character but are NOT Heritage Conservation Areas
3. Precinct provisions (including heritage-related ones) apply to the **entire precinct boundary**, not just HCA portions

### Legal Authority Chain:

```
EP&A Act s 3.42
    ↓
"DCPs supplement LEPs and apply to mapped areas"
    ↓
DCP Precinct Maps define geographic scope
    ↓
Precinct provisions apply to ALL properties within precinct boundaries
    ↓
Including heritage-related provisions about character, streetscape, etc.
```

### Supporting Evidence:

**1. Statutory Framework**
- EP&A Act s 3.42 explicitly allows DCPs to apply controls based on mapped areas
- DCP precinct maps are statutory instruments that define scope
- Council has authority to create precinct-specific controls for character management

**2. Precinct Descriptions**
Example from Leichhardt DCP - Excelsior Estate Distinctive Neighbourhood (C2.2.3):
> "The Thornley Sub Area (C2.2.3.1) is a small section of Heritage Conservation Area **within** the Excelsior Estate Distinctive Neighbourhood"

This shows:
- The precinct (Excelsior Estate) is LARGER than the HCA (Thornley)
- But provisions apply to the entire precinct, not just the HCA portion

**3. Council Practice**
- Councils consistently enforce precinct controls across entire precinct areas
- DA assessment applies precinct provisions based on address/location, not LEP heritage status
- Heritage advisors assess against precinct provisions for all properties in precinct

## Practical Examples

### Example 1: Non-Heritage Property in Heritage Precinct
**Address**: Hypothetical property at 50 Johnston Street, Annandale (NOT in HCA)

**LEP Status**: ❌ NOT a Heritage Item, NOT in Heritage Conservation Area

**DCP Precinct**: C2.2.1.3 (Johnston Street/Witches Houses Distinctive Neighbourhood)

**Receives**:
- ❌ NO generic heritage provisions (Part C Section 1.4) - requires LEP heritage status
- ✅ YES precinct heritage provisions (Part C Section 2) - 4 provisions about:
  - Maintaining Victorian terrace character
  - Respecting streetscape
  - Protecting contributory buildings
  - Preserving heritage setting

**Why?**
- The property is within the Johnston Street/Witches Houses precinct boundary
- The precinct has identified heritage character (Victorian terraces)
- EP&A Act s 3.42 allows the DCP to apply precinct controls to ALL properties in the precinct
- The provisions protect the **precinct's heritage character**, not individual heritage items

### Example 2: Heritage Property in Heritage Precinct
**Address**: 260 Johnston Street, Annandale (IN HCA)

**LEP Status**: ✅ In Heritage Conservation Area (C44 - Annandale HCA)

**DCP Precinct**: C2.2.1.3 (Johnston Street/Witches Houses Distinctive Neighbourhood)

**Receives**:
- ✅ YES generic heritage provisions (Part C Section 1.4) - 53 provisions
- ✅ YES precinct heritage provisions (Part C Section 2) - 4 provisions
- **Total**: 57 heritage provisions

**Why?**
- LEP heritage status triggers generic DCP heritage provisions
- Precinct location triggers precinct-specific provisions
- Property must comply with BOTH sets of controls

### Example 3: Heritage Property NOT in Heritage Precinct
**Address**: 185 Parramatta Road, Annandale (IN HCA)

**LEP Status**: ✅ In Heritage Conservation Area (C35 - Parramatta Road HCA)

**DCP Precinct**: C2.2.1.7 (Parramatta Road Commercial) - NO heritage provisions in this precinct

**Receives**:
- ✅ YES generic heritage provisions (Part C Section 1.4) - 53 provisions
- ❌ NO precinct heritage provisions - this precinct has no heritage-related provisions
- **Total**: 53 heritage provisions

**Why?**
- LEP heritage status triggers generic DCP heritage provisions
- This precinct is commercial character, not heritage character
- Precinct has no heritage-specific provisions to apply

## Key Distinctions

### Heritage Conservation Area (HCA) vs Heritage Precinct

| Aspect | Heritage Conservation Area | Heritage Precinct |
|--------|---------------------------|-------------------|
| **Defined by** | Inner West LEP 2022 | Inner West DCP |
| **Authority** | LEP Clause 5.10 | EP&A Act s 3.42 + DCP precinct maps |
| **Shown on** | LEP Heritage Map | DCP Precinct Maps |
| **Triggers** | Generic heritage provisions | Precinct-specific provisions |
| **Scope** | Properties within HCA boundary | ALL properties in precinct boundary |
| **Purpose** | Identify areas of heritage significance | Manage precinct character (may include heritage) |
| **Can overlap?** | YES - an HCA can be within a precinct | YES - a precinct can contain an HCA |

### Generic Heritage Provisions vs Precinct Heritage Provisions

| Aspect | Generic Heritage (Part C §1.4) | Precinct Heritage (Part C §2) |
|--------|-------------------------------|------------------------------|
| **Apply when** | LEP heritage status = YES | Property in precinct boundary |
| **Number** | 53 provisions (Leichhardt) | 0-5 per precinct (varies) |
| **Content** | Broad heritage principles | Precinct-specific character |
| **Authority** | LEP Clause 5.10 | EP&A Act s 3.42 + precinct maps |
| **Examples** | "Retain original fabric", "Sympathetic materials" | "Maintain Victorian terrace rhythm", "Protect sandstone features" |

## How This Is Implemented in the System

### Database Structure

```sql
-- Generic heritage provisions
SELECT * FROM regulatory_provisions
WHERE v2_dcp_layer = 'generic'
  AND (v2_marker = 'heritage' OR v2_topic = 'heritage');
-- Returns provisions that require LEP heritage status

-- Precinct heritage provisions
SELECT * FROM regulatory_provisions
WHERE v2_dcp_layer = 'precinct'
  AND v2_precinct_id = 'C2.2.1.3'
  AND (v2_marker = 'heritage' OR v2_topic = 'heritage');
-- Returns provisions for ALL properties in precinct C2.2.1.3
```

### API Filtering Logic

```typescript
// frontend-nextjs/app/api/provisions/for-property/route.ts (lines 557-574)

if (!filters.heritage && layer !== 'condition' && layer !== 'precinct') {
  // Property is NOT heritage
  // Exclude heritage provisions from generic/use-specific layers
  sql += ` AND (LOWER(v2_topic) != 'heritage' AND (v2_marker IS NULL OR v2_marker != 'heritage'))`;
}

// Note: Precinct layer (layer === 'precinct') is NOT filtered by heritage status
// Precinct provisions (including heritage-tagged ones) apply to ALL properties in precinct
// Authority: EP&A Act s 3.42 - precinct maps define scope independently of LEP schedules
```

### Property Data Flow

```
1. User enters address
   ↓
2. Geocode to coordinates
   ↓
3. NSW Planning Portal API
   ↓
4. Returns: zone, LEP heritage status, precinct ID
   ↓
5. Query provisions API with filters:
   - heritage: true/false (from LEP)
   - precinct_id: "C2.2.1.3" (from DCP precinct map)
   ↓
6. Apply filtering logic:
   - Generic heritage: Only if heritage = true
   - Precinct heritage: Always if in precinct_id
   ↓
7. Return provisions to user
```

## Summary

### Key Points:

1. **LEP defines heritage** (Schedule 5, Heritage Map)
2. **DCP provides controls** (generic + precinct-specific)
3. **Generic heritage provisions** apply based on LEP heritage status
4. **Precinct heritage provisions** apply based on precinct boundaries (EP&A Act s 3.42)
5. **Precincts ≠ Heritage Conservation Areas** (different maps, different authority)
6. **Properties can receive heritage provisions even if NOT in HCA** (if in heritage precinct)

### The Critical Finding from Perplexity:

> EP&A Act s 3.42 establishes that DCPs supplement LEPs and apply to mapped areas. DCP precinct maps define scope **independently of LEP heritage schedules**. Therefore, precinct provisions (including heritage-related ones) apply to ALL properties within precinct boundaries, regardless of LEP heritage status.

This is not a bug or workaround - it's the **intended operation of the NSW planning system** where DCPs provide detailed controls for character management at a precinct level, supplementing (but separate from) LEP heritage protections.
