# User Story: Town Planner DA Preparation — Dual Occupancy in Heritage Area
## Ashfield R2 Low Density Residential Zone

**Last Updated:** 2026-02-05 (tested against production API)

---

## The User

**Emma Roberts, Senior Town Planner**
- Works at boutique planning consultancy (8-10 planners)
- 7 years experience in residential DA preparation
- Prepares 3-5 Development Applications per week
- Specializes in Inner West Council DAs (Ashfield, Leichhardt, Marrickville)
- Bills clients $150-300/hour for DA preparation work
- Under constant deadline pressure (DAs have lodgment deadlines, client expectations)

**Pain point:** DA report preparation requires citing 50-100+ DCP provisions with exact PDF references. Finding and copying these provisions from fragmented DCPs takes 4-6 hours per DA. Faster DA prep = higher throughput = more revenue.

---

## The Job

**Client:** Property owner seeking approval for dual occupancy on R2 site in Ashfield heritage area

**Development Application scope:**
- Demolish existing rear garage
- Construct two new two-storey dwellings (Dwelling A: 3 bed, Dwelling B: 2 bed)
- Total floor area: 320 sqm (within FSR limit)
- Car parking: 4 spaces (2 per dwelling) in attached garages
- Landscaping: 40% of site (exceeds minimum)
- Heritage area: must demonstrate heritage compatibility

**DA Report sections requiring DCP provision citations:**
1. **Built Form** (setbacks, height, building envelope) — 10-15 provisions
2. **Parking & Access** (car parking rates, driveway design) — 5-10 provisions
3. **Landscaping** (deep soil, tree preservation, landscaped area) — 8-12 provisions
4. **Heritage Impact Statement** (Materials, Additions, Demolition, Character, Fencing) — 20-30 provisions
5. **Privacy & Amenity** (privacy screens, solar access, overlooking) — 4-6 provisions
6. **Waste Management** (bin storage, collection access) — 3-5 provisions
7. **Stormwater** (drainage, detention, OSD) — 2-4 provisions

**Total provisions to cite:** 60-80 provisions across 7 DA report sections

**Lodgment deadline:** 3 weeks from client engagement (standard for boutique firms)

## The Pain Point (Manual Process)

### Step 1: Download Ashfield DCP (15 minutes)
1. Navigate to Inner West Council website
2. Find DCP page for former Ashfield council area
3. Identify which PDFs contain relevant provisions:
   - **Part A:** Introduction (skip — no actionable provisions)
   - **Part B:** Residential Design (setbacks, building design, privacy)
   - **Part C:** Heritage Conservation (heritage controls — critical for this DA)
   - **Part D:** Parking and Access (parking rates, driveway design)
   - **Part F:** Development Category Guidelines (R2 zone-specific controls)
   - **Chapter A:** Miscellaneous (landscaping, waste, stormwater)

4. Download 6 separate PDFs (total 200+ pages, 80+ MB)
5. Save to project folder: `DA-2026-045-Ashfield-Dual-Occ/DCP-References/`

**Common issues:**
- Council website links sometimes broken (cached PDFs from previous projects used instead)
- DCP amended over time — need to verify correct version (as of DA lodgment date)
- Part names don't clearly indicate content (is landscaping in Part B or Chapter A?)

---

### Step 2: Find setback and building envelope provisions (30 minutes)

#### 2a. Search Part B: Residential Design
1. Open Part B PDF (50 pages)
2. No table of contents with "setbacks" entry
3. Ctrl+F search "rear setback" → 8 results
4. Skim through results to find R2 zone-specific provision
5. Find: "Rear setback for dwelling houses in R2: minimum 6 meters"
6. **Copy into DA report template:**
   > "The proposed dual occupancy complies with Ashfield DCP Part B, Section 4.2, page 18: rear setback of 6 meters provided for both dwellings."
7. **Manually note:** Part B, page 18 (for PDF hyperlink later)

#### 2b. Check Part F for R2-specific controls
1. Open Part F PDF (40 pages)
2. Navigate to "Low Density Residential" section
3. Cross-check rear setback: confirms 6m (same as Part B)
4. Find additional control: "Upper floor setback from side boundaries: minimum 2m"
5. **Copy into DA report:**
   > "Upper floor side setbacks comply with Part F, page 7: 2 meters provided on both sides."
6. **Manually note:** Part F, page 7

#### 2c. Find height and solar access controls
1. Still in Part B, search "height" → 23 results
2. Find solar access plane diagram (2D diagram, page 22)
3. Find provision: "3m height at boundary, 45 degree slope to north"
4. **Copy into DA report:**
   > "Solar access planes comply with Part B, page 22: second storey roof setback 1.5m from north boundary to comply with 3m + 45° plane."
5. **Manually note:** Part B, page 22

**Time so far: 45 minutes**

**Common issues:**
- Setback provisions scattered across Part B and Part F (easy to miss one)
- Upper floor setbacks sometimes different from ground floor (ambiguous language)
- Solar access diagrams are 2D (hard to apply to actual site orientation)

#### 3b. Find driveway design provisions
1. Still in Part D, search "driveway" → 12 results
2. Find provision: "Driveway width: minimum 3m for dual driveway access"
3. Find provision: "Driveway gradient: maximum 1:4 (25%)"
4. **Copy into DA report:**
   > "Driveway design complies with Part D, pages 12-13: 3.2m width, 1:6 gradient."
5. **Manually note:** Part D, pages 12-13

#### 3c. Check for SEPP Housing cross-references
1. Note in Part D: "Parking may be reduced under SEPP Housing 2021 if within 800m of public transport"
2. Emma must check if site qualifies → manually measure distance on Google Maps

**Time so far: 70 minutes (1 hour 10 min)**

**Common issues:**

- SEPP Housing cross-reference requires separate calculation (manual map measurement)
- Driveway gradient provision buried in text (not in table — easy to miss)

### Step 4: Find landscaping and tree preservation provisions (35 minutes)

#### 4b. Find tree preservation controls
1. Still in Chapter A, search "tree" → 34 results
2. Find section: "Tree Preservation"
3. Site has 2 existing trees (Eucalyptus, Corymbia)
4. Find provision: "Trees with trunk diameter >200mm require arborist report if within 5m of construction"

**Time so far: 105 minutes (1 hour 45 min)**

**Common issues:**

5. **Copy into DA report:**
   > "Design approach per Part C, page 25: Contemporary architecture with heritage-compatible materials (brick, tiles) and scale (single-storey street presentation, two-storey at rear)."
6. **Manually note:** Part C, pages 24-25

#### 5d. Copy Demolition provisions
1. Navigate to "Demolition" section (4 pages)
2. Find provision: "Demolition of non-contributory structures permitted"
3. **Copy into DA report:**
   > "Demolition complies with Part C, page 30: Existing rear garage (c.1980, non-contributory) proposed for removal. Original c.1920 dwelling retained."

#### 5e. Copy Character and Fencing provisions
1. Find Character provision: "New development must respect rhythm of detached dwellings in streetscape"
2. **Copy into DA report:**
   > "Streetscape character addressed per Part C, page 35: Proposed dual occupancy maintains detached dwelling rhythm with 6m spacing between dwellings, consistent with heritage pattern."
3. Find Fencing provision: "Front fences maximum 1.2m height, open style (pickets or wrought iron)"
4. **Copy into DA report:**
   > "Front fencing complies with Part C, page 38: Proposed 1.2m timber picket fence matches heritage fencing pattern."
5. **Manually note:** Part C, pages 35, 38

**Time so far: 165 minutes (2 hours 45 min)**

**Common issues:**
- Heritage provisions are 50+ pages (most time-consuming section)
- Provisions overlap (Materials mentions "compatible," Additions mentions "compatible" — are these the same standard?)
- Subjective language ("subservient," "compatible," "respectful") — Emma interprets conservatively to avoid rejection
- Missing one heritage provision = council rejection or deferred determination (3-4 week delay)
