## Understanding NSW Planning Hierarchy

**SEPP (State Environmental Planning Policy):**
- State-wide legislation
- Made under Environmental Planning & Assessment Act 1979
- OVERRIDES local plans when there's conflict
- Examples: SEPP Housing 2021, SEPP Transport 2021

**LEP (Local Environmental Plan):**
- LOCAL LEGISLATION for each council area
- Legally binding, enforceable in court
- Sets zoning, land use permissions, FSR, height limits
- Must comply with SEPPs (State overrides Local)
- Example: Inner West LEP 2022

**DCP (Development Control Plan):**
- GUIDELINES, not legislation
- Provides design standards and detailed controls
- Consent authorities "must take into consideration" (not must comply)
- Examples: Marrickville DCP 2011, Ashfield DCP 2016

**Legal hierarchy:** SEPP > LEP > DCP
**Your advantage:** You have ALL THREE layers extracted and structured
