# B2C Go-To-Market Plan — Most Profitable, Scalable, Manageable

**Date:** 2026-06-13
**Branch:** `claude/b2c-niche-research-pd4qbv`
**Builds on:** `B2C_NICHE_RESEARCH_2026.md` (Niche A — *Development Watch*, recurring; Niche B —
*Was it approved?*, one-off).
**Audience:** founder-led, small team. Every tactic here is chosen to be runnable by 1–2 people
and to compound rather than to require constant spend.

**The one-line answer to "what's the most profitable plan":**
> Build **one free, address-level "Property Risk Snapshot"** page. Make it the SEO engine, the ad
> landing page, *and* the widget partners embed. It teases a finding → sells the **one-off report
> (B, cash today)** → converts the buyer into the **recurring subscription (A, cash forever)**.
> Acquire through **conveyancer / buyer's-agent partnerships** (near-zero CAC), not paid ads.

---

## 1. The profit flywheel (the whole strategy in one loop)

```
        Programmatic SEO + partner widgets  ──►  FREE address "Risk Snapshot"
                       ▲                                     │  (teaser finding)
                       │                                     ▼
            reviews, backlinks, word-of-mouth      Product B: "Was it approved?"  ($99 one-off, cash today)
                       ▲                                     │
                       │                                     ▼
        every customer = more SEO authority   ◄──  Product A: "Development Watch" ($49/yr, recurring)
```

Why this is the most profitable shape:
- **Acquisition cost trends to zero.** SEO and embedded partner widgets are owned channels — once
  built they keep delivering without per-lead spend. Paid ads (CAC ~$66–100/lead in AU real
  estate) are used *only* to validate and fill gaps, never as the engine.
- **Cash now + cash forever.** B funds the business from day one (high-margin one-off at peak
  buyer anxiety); A compounds into ARR. You are never dependent on a single transaction.
- **One asset, every channel.** The free Snapshot is simultaneously the SEO page, the paid-ad
  destination, and the partner embed — so every dollar of build effort is reused three ways.

## 2. The single best promotional asset — the free "Property Risk Snapshot"

This is the highest-leverage thing to build, because it is the front of *every* channel.

- **What it is:** type any NSW address → instant free page showing zone, height, flood/bushfire,
  heritage from free Planning Portal data (the **Property Profile Hub already exists** at
  `/property?address=`). Then a **teaser line** that creates the itch:
  > *"⚠️ We detected 1 structural change at this address since 2017 with no matching approval
  > record on the public register. Unlock the full Title-Check report → $99."*
  and
  > *"3 development applications were lodged within 200 m in the last 12 months. Get alerted on
  > the next one → free."*
- **Why it's irresistible:** it names a specific, scary, *unresolved* fact about *this exact
  house* — the textbook trigger for a paid unlock. Loss-aversion does the selling.
- **Why it's the most profitable promo:** it is **programmatic-SEO-ready**. Generate one page per
  address/suburb/street (Zillow and Flyhomes scaled this to hundreds of thousands of pages; we
  already have the template and free data). Each page is a free, compounding, long-tail organic
  entry point ("is 14 Smith St approved", "what's being built near [suburb]") at near-zero
  marginal cost. It is also the exact page partners embed and ads point to.

**Guardrail:** the teaser must stay factual — *"change detected, no matching approval located,
verify with council"* — never *"illegal works"*. (See §10.)

## 4. Partnership playbook (the highest-leverage lever)

Partnerships put us in front of the buyer **at the exact moment of need, with someone they
already trust paying or recommending us** — the lowest-CAC acquisition possible. NSW supply is
deep: **~1,700 conveyancers AU**, **~40,950 solicitors in NSW**, **~14,329 real estate agents /
3,909 agencies in NSW**, **1,000+ buyer's agents AU**.

### 4.1 Priority order
1. **Conveyancers & property solicitors** — they *already* flag unapproved-works risk in every
   contract review but **cannot detect it**. We complete their job. Embed Product B as a one-click
   "order Title-Check" inside their workflow.
2. **Buyer's agents** — paid to do due diligence; white-label B as part of their service.
3. **Mortgage brokers** — unapproved works threaten the loan; they have a self-interested reason
   to surface it before valuation.
4. **Real estate agency networks** — give buyers the free Snapshot + discounted A as a value-add
   (also softens vendor disclosure).

### 4.2 The deal structure (keep it simple & standard)
- **Referral / white-label, 20–30% revenue share** (30% is the common AU white-label rate) — or a
  flat per-report wholesale price they mark up. Choose rev-share for transparency.
- **Co-branded report** (their logo on the PDF) for white-label partners — increases their
  perceived value, locks them in.
- **One-page agreement** (a standard revenue-share agreement template) — no bespoke legal per
  partner. Manageable at scale.

### 4.3 The pitch (B, to a conveyancer)
> *"You already warn clients about unapproved works — but you can't see them. We compare 8 years
> of satellite imagery against the public approval register and flag structures with no consent on
> record, per address, in minutes. Embed a 'Check this property' button on your site. You earn 30%
> per report and look like the firm that caught the $200k problem before settlement."*

### 4.4 Onboarding (extendable, low-touch)
- Self-serve partner portal: sign up → get an embed snippet + referral code → dashboard of
  reports & earnings. (Reuses the existing Stripe + report pipeline.)
- Start with **10 hand-picked Inner West / Eastern Suburbs conveyancers** (where LGA data is
  already live), prove the motion, then template the outreach and scale to the long tail.

## 5. Advertising / paid plan (the thermostat)

Use paid to **validate messaging and capture peak intent**, not to carry growth.

| Channel | Product | Intent | Starting budget | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Google Search | B | "is [address] approved", "unapproved works check", "pre-purchase [suburb]" | $2–3k/mo (AU min for signal) | CPL <$80 → report margin positive | Auction-week + weekend buyer intent is gold |
| Google Search | A | "what's being built near me", "object to development [suburb]" | $1k/mo | Email capture (free tier) | Cheap; CPC ~$2.53 in AU real estate |
| Meta geo-targeted | A | suburb-level, DA-hotspot postcodes | $1k/mo | $/free-signup, then convert | Hyperlocal creative: "A 2-storey was just filed on [street]" |
| Retargeting (Meta/Google) | A+B | Snapshot visitors who didn't convert | $500/mo | Recover abandoners | Always-on, cheapest ROI |

**Rule:** a paid channel stays on only while *(LTV per acquired customer) > 3 × (CAC on that
channel)*. With B at $99 and ~$66–100 CPL, paid-to-B is **break-even-ish on the first sale** — it
only becomes profitable once you count the **A subscription conversion + repeat referrals**. So
paid is justified *because* of the flywheel, not on its own.

## 6. Funnel, pricing & promotional mechanics

### Funnel
`Free Snapshot (SEO/ad/widget)` → `teaser finding` → `Product B unlock $99` → `post-settlement
upsell to Product A $49/yr` → `referral / review loop`.

### Pricing & packaging
| Offer | Price | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Property Risk Snapshot | **Free** | Lead magnet + SEO engine |
| **B — Title-Check** ("Was it approved?") | **$99** one-off (test $79 vs $129) | Cash front door |
| **A — Development Watch (Sunlight Guard)** | **$49/yr** (free raw-alert tier below) | Recurring engine |
| **Bundle: Buyer's Peace-of-Mind** | **$129** (B + first year of A) | Maximises LTV at point of sale |
| C — Future-Proof climate add-on | +$29 inside B | Margin upsell |

### Promotional mechanics that lift profit without discounting the core
- **Conditional teaser** — only show the "$99 unlock" prompt when a finding *exists*; when it
  doesn't, pivot to the free A signup. Never waste the moment.
- **Settlement-day convert** — automated email to every B buyer: *"You bought it — now keep watch
  over it. First year of Development Watch for $29."*
- **Refer-a-buyer** — give B customers a shareable discount; buyers cluster (same agent, same
  street) so referrals compound.
- **Partner co-brand** — partner's logo on the report; their clients trust it more, they push it
  harder.
- **Annual prepay for A** — bill yearly, not monthly: better cash flow, lower churn, less admin.

## 7. Unit economics & leverage model (illustrative)

Assumptions kept conservative; tune with real data.

- **Product B:** $99 price, ~$5 cloud/QA cost → **~$94 contribution**. Via partner (30% share) →
  **~$64 net**, at ~$0 CAC. Via paid ($80 CPL) → **~$14 first-sale** (the flywheel must recover
  the rest).
- **Product A:** $49/yr, near-zero serve cost, ~$45 contribution/yr. Assume 3-yr avg life →
  **~$135 LTV**. Even a 20% B→A conversion adds ~$27 of A-LTV to every B sale.
- **Blended LTV per acquired buyer** (B + 20% × A-LTV) ≈ **$99 + $27 ≈ $126**, much of it at ~$0
  CAC through partners/SEO.
- **Target ratio:** LTV:CAC **> 3:1** on owned/partner channels (easily met); paid channels held
  to payback **< first sale + 1 A renewal**.
- **Illustrative scale:** 1% of 186k NSW settlements = 1,860 B sales/yr ≈ **$120–175k** + the
  compounding A book. Both run on pipelines that already exist.

## 8. Phased rollout — manageable & extendable

Each phase has a **gate**: don't start the next until the gate is met. This keeps it runnable by a
small team and prevents over-building.

### Phase 0 — Foundation (Weeks 1–4)
- Ship the **free Snapshot** with the conditional teaser (front of every channel).
- Stand up **Product B checkout** end-to-end (pipeline exists; add the "change with no matching
  approval" reconciliation flag + consumer framing).
- Write the **partner one-pager + standard 30% rev-share agreement** + embeddable widget snippet.
- **Gate:** 20 real Snapshots generated; B report renders correctly for 10 known addresses.

### Phase 1 — Cash via partners (Weeks 5–12)
- Sign **10 Inner West / Eastern Suburbs conveyancers + 3 buyer's agents** (data already live).
- Turn on **Google Search (B)** at $2–3k/mo to validate messaging in parallel.
- Launch **programmatic SEO** for live LGAs (one page per suburb/street first, addresses next).
- **Gate:** 50 paid B reports; ≥1 partner sending repeat volume; CPL < $80.

### Phase 2 — Recurring engine (Months 4–6)
- Launch **Product A** (free raw alert + $49 impact tier) with **settlement-day upsell** from B.
- Add **Meta geo-targeting (A)** in DA-hotspot postcodes + retargeting.
- Recruit **mortgage brokers + 1 agency network**.
- **Gate:** 500 free-A signups; 15% free→paid A conversion; 20% B→A conversion.

### Phase 3 — Scale & extend (Months 7–12)
- Expand pSEO + partners **as each new LGA goes live** (TAM grows automatically with the B2B
  roadmap).
- Add **insurers / strata** (climate + unapproved-works); bundle **C** as upsell.
- Hire/outsource partner success once partner revenue > paid spend.
- **Gate:** partner channel > paid channel in volume; A ARR covers fixed costs.

## 9. The recommendation (most profitable single plan)

1. **Build the free Snapshot once** — it is SEO page + ad landing + partner widget in one.
2. **Lead with Product B through conveyancer/buyer's-agent partnerships** — cash now, ~$0 CAC,
   trusted-advisor distribution.
3. **Convert every B buyer to Product A at settlement** — turn one-off cash into recurring ARR.
4. **Let programmatic SEO compound underneath everything** — the moat that makes CAC fall over
   time.
5. **Use paid only as a thermostat** to validate and fill gaps.

This is profitable from the first partner, scalable to every NSW address, manageable by a founder
+ contractor, and extendable LGA-by-LGA in lockstep with the existing B2B expansion — no new data
science required, only integration and distribution.

## 10. Guardrails (per CLAUDE.md — non-negotiable)

- **Factual framing only.** *"Structural change detected c.2021; no matching consent located on
  the public register — verify with council."* Never *"illegal/unapproved/unsafe"*; never a legal
  conclusion or approval prediction.
- **No hardcoded regulatory values** in any promo, page, or report — controls cited (e.g. in
  shadow/impact packs) must come from the authoritative live source.
- **Keep internal pipeline names out of all consumer-facing and partner-facing copy** (the
  IP-protection rule) — use generic product names (Title-Check, Development Watch).
- **Source + date on every fact; flag uncertainty** rather than smoothing it.
- Partner agreements: standard revenue-share template, GST-inclusive pricing, clear data-use
  terms.
